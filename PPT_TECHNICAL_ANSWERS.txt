Q1: How does your system handle massive drone imagery files without crashing standard web browsers?
====================================================================================================

[BUILT]
We implemented a Chunk-Based GeoTIFF Streaming Engine directly in our architecture.
This engine utilizes Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF (COG) protocols and browser-based Web Workers to divide massive 500MB+ imagery files.
These files are chunked into dynamic spatial tiles for seamless client-side streaming and data ingestion.
This streaming framework prevents standard desktop GIS software and web browsers from crashing or freezing during inspection.
It ensures fluid 60fps rendering of 2D overhead orthomosaics and 3D volumetric building models without UI lag.
Municipal officers can access the platform on standard browsers without heavy installations or expensive per-seat GIS licenses.
Our integrated pre-cached datasets also allow instant, single-click evaluation during live jury demonstrations.

[ROADMAP]
Moving forward, we will enhance this data ingestion pipeline with multi-sensor data fusion to handle dense visual obstructions.
We plan to combine regular RGB drone photos with LIDAR laser scanning and ground-level camera checks.

We will build direct API connectors with the National Land Records Modernization Programme (NLRMP).

Our containerized cloud architecture is fully prepared to scale across multiple urban wards and enterprise city deployments.

Q2: How accurate is your boundary detection, and how do you handle flawed legacy paper maps or complex buildings?
==================================================================================================================
